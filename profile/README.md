# Telarsa

**A technology company building digital products.**

Telarsa builds digital products and infrastructure that make connections
between people, businesses and opportunities simpler, smarter and more
dependable. Persian-first, by design: our products are built for readers whose
script, digits, calendar and reading direction most software gets wrong.

## How we work

- **Measured, not assumed.** A claim about a product is a number from its build output, not a screenshot.
- **Correct in the reader's language first.** Persian digits, the Jalali calendar and an announced name in the reader's script are requirements, not localisation polish.
- **Small, sharp tools over large frameworks.** Components come from the libraries other people maintain; we ship the layer those libraries cannot be.
